**Who:** a visitor, logged out. Walk every page in English and in Arabic.

**Screens:**

- Blog list `/kafala-blogs`: the published blogs show; the draft must not
- A blog `/kafala-blogs/<id>`: open several from the cards; also try an id that doesn't exist
- Article list `/kafala-articles` and an article `/kafala-articles/<id>`
- FAQs `/faqs`: open and close every question; only published ones show; the search box (a word that matches, one that doesn't, clearing it) and the "show all" button
- Resources `/resources`: each card's link (it opens the document in a new tab); a resource's address `/resources/<slug>` (it redirects to the document; the demo's slugs are `annual-report-2024-25` and `dar-ifta-fatwa-source`); a slug that doesn't exist

**Look hard at:** a story that shows the other language's text, headings and lists inside the rich text, pictures that are too large or cut off at 360 px, cards that don't line up right to left in Arabic, links back to the list, pages that stay on a loading state.
