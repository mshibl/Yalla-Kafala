# Reference sites for the design council

The design council (`pnpm design-council`) scores our screenshots side by side with screenshots of other sites. A reviewer that sees our site alone scores it too generously; next to strong sites, the scores become honest, and "better than these" becomes the goal.

Put each site's screenshots in a folder of its own here, named after the site:

```
design/references/
  sos-childrens-villages/
    home-1440.png
    home-390.png
    donate-390.png
  charity-water/
    home-1440.png
```

- Take them yourself, at phone width (390 px) and laptop width (1440 px), of the pages that match ours: the home page, an explainer page, the steps of a process, donate. An Arabic site among them helps the Arabic score.
- Three to six screenshots per site, two or three sites. More pictures make each review shallower.
- Pick sites you would be glad to be compared with: a child-welfare or fostering organisation, a charity known for its design, and an Arabic-language site done well.

Everything here except this file is git-ignored: other organisations' screenshots stay on your machine. The folder is read from the checkout you run the council in.
